# RunIntervals Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

This policy describes the current RunIntervals beta for Garmin Forerunner 970.

## Data used by the app

RunIntervals uses the watch's location and heart-rate interfaces to record a running activity. When available, the activity includes GPS coordinates, distance, speed, heart rate, timing, laps and workout phase information. Location and heart rate may be unavailable or inaccurate, particularly indoors.

The app records this information locally on the Garmin device in a FIT activity file. It does not require a separate RunIntervals account.

## Transmission and developer access

RunIntervals does not send workout data to a server operated by the developer. It has no external analytics, advertising or tracking service, and does not automatically give the developer access to recorded activities.

Garmin software may synchronize activities to Garmin Connect according to your Garmin account and device settings. Garmin's handling of synchronized data is described in the [Garmin Connect privacy policy](https://www.garmin.com/en-US/privacy/connect/). If you connect Garmin to another service, that service's privacy practices also apply to transfers you authorize.

If you voluntarily send an activity file or diagnostic information to the developer for support, it may contain health and precise location information. Share only the information you intend to disclose.

## Storage and control

Activities remain on your device and, if synchronized, in your Garmin account until removed using the controls available there. Removing the RunIntervals app does not necessarily remove previously recorded activities. You can manage or delete activities using your Garmin device or Garmin Connect, and stop future app recording by ending the workout or uninstalling the app.

## Permissions

- Fit: create and save running activities.
- FitContributor: add workout phase, target and result fields to the activity.
- Positioning: request GPS location data.
- Sensor: request heart-rate data.

## Contact and updates

For questions about this beta, use the support contact listed on its Connect IQ page. This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change.
